The Material

Iridescent
thermo-weave.

A membrane-thin fabric engineered to bend light at the fold. It reads black at rest and blooms into spectral colour as it moves with the body — a second skin that never wears the same twice.

  • CompositionRecycled thermo-filament · 0.4mm
  • FinishThin-film iridescent coating
  • WeightFeather · 120 g/m²

The Atelier

From a single seam to a second skin.

01

Draft in shadow

Every silhouette begins as a light study — we model how fabric folds swallow and release light before a single cut is made.

02

Weave to order

The thermo-weave is spun on demand, tuned to your measurements. Nothing is stocked; nothing is wasted.

03

Finish by hand

Each seam is closed by a single maker and signed. Your piece carries their mark and its own serial.
