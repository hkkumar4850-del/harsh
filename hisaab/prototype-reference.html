Kya mera bank data safe hai?

Haan. SMS data aapke phone pe hi process hota hai, encrypted form mein. Hum aapka data kisi ko bechte nahi — kabhi nahi. Aap ek tap mein apna saara data delete kar sakte ho.

Kya yeh app investment advice deta hai?

Hisaab ek financial education aur planning tool hai. Hum general guidance dete hain — jaise SIP calculation aur tax planning ke options. Specific stocks ya funds ki personalized advice ke liye SEBI-registered advisor se consult karein.

Mujhe English nahi aati. Chalega?

Bilkul chalega — isi liye toh Hisaab banaya hai! App ka har screen, har notification, har coach ka jawaab aapki chuni hui bhasha mein hoga.

Free plan mein kya milta hai?

Auto expense tracking, monthly report, 1 goal, aur coach se 5 sawaal har mahine — hamesha ke liye free. Jab lage ki aur chahiye, tab upgrade karo.

Subscription cancel kaise karein?

App ke settings mein ek button hai — "Cancel karo". Bas. Koi call nahi, koi email nahi, koi "are you sure" ka jaal nahi.
